2. Enter the Game

Choose your stake, then submit your purchase details.

Each entry requires a new qualifying MACO buy. Existing holdings alone do not qualify. Your purchase must be indexed and verified before your entry can be accepted.

How It Works

  1. Select one of the six stake levels.
  2. Make a new MACO purchase worth at least the selected stake.
  3. Choose a number between 1 and 10.
  4. Submit your wallet address and purchase transaction hash.
  5. Each stake level has its own pot of 11 entries.
  6. The closest guess to the winning number wins. If guesses are equally close, the earliest accepted entry wins.
  7. The winner's prize is 50% of the selected stake.

Entries are subject to transaction verification and the game's published rules. Do not submit the same purchase transaction twice.
