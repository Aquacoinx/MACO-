Enter the Game

Use the hash of an eligible MACO buy transaction on Robinhood Chain. Minimum qualifying purchase: $10.

How It Works

  1. Make an eligible MACO purchase worth at least $10.
  2. Choose one number between 1 and 10.
  3. Submit your wallet address and transaction hash.
  4. Each qualifying purchase can count as one entry.
  5. Each pot contains 11 entries.
  6. The exact winning guess wins $5.
  7. If nobody guesses exactly, the closest guess wins.
  8. If equally close guesses tie, the earliest verified entry wins.

Winners are subject to verification. Prizes are paid manually. This page does not transfer funds automatically.
