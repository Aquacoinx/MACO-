How to Play

  1. Make a new $MACO purchase worth at least $10.
  2. Enter the wallet that made the purchase.
  3. Submit its transaction hash and choose a number from 1 to 10.
  4. Each qualifying transaction can only be used once.
  5. After 11 verified entries, the pot closes.
  6. An exact guess wins. If nobody guesses exactly, the closest guess wins.
  7. If guesses are equally close, the earliest verified entry wins.
  8. The winner receives $5 through manual payout.
Never share your seed phrase or private key. Only purchases verified by the game backend qualify.
