1. Getting Started

To participate in the MACO Volume Arena, you need:

  • An EVM-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or OKX Wallet.
  • ETH on Robinhood Chain.
  • Access to the MACO trading platform.

2. How to Register

Step 1: Open the MACO Volume Arena website using your wallet's in-app browser.
Step 2: Click the REGISTER button.
Step 3: Connect your wallet.
Step 4: Sign the registration message in your wallet.
Step 5: Your wallet is registered for the contest.

Registration uses a wallet signature. Only sign the registration request you initiated. Never share your recovery phrase or private keys.

4. How Trading Volume Works

The Volume Arena tracks recorded MACO trading activity on Robinhood Chain.

  • Buy Volume: Total recorded value of eligible purchases.
  • Sell Volume: Total recorded value of eligible sales.
  • Total Volume: Combined buy and sell volume.

Trading volume is indexed before it appears on the leaderboard. Allow time for transactions to be processed.

5. Referral Program

Invite other traders and grow your network.

Step 1: Open the Referrals section on the Volume Arena.
Step 2: Get your personal referral code or referral link.
Step 3: Share it with friends, communities and other traders.
Step 4: Encourage them to register and participate.

When new participants register through your referral, your referral activity can be tracked by the platform.

6. Leaderboard and Rankings

The leaderboard displays participant rankings based on recorded trading volume.

  • Wallet address
  • Buy volume
  • Sell volume
  • Total volume
  • Rank position

Use the search feature to find a wallet and refresh the leaderboard to view updated records.

7. Trade Fairly

  • Use your own registered wallet.
  • Make genuine trading transactions.
  • Never share your wallet recovery phrase.
  • Allow transactions to be indexed.
  • Follow official contest announcements for reward and payout conditions.
